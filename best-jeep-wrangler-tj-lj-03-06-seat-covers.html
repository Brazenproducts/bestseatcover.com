TJ/LJ Fitment Overview

2003 introduced the Unlimited (LJ), a long-wheelbase TJ variant sold through 2006. Seat shape carried over from the earlier TJ with minor trim differences. Most TJ/LJ trims lack side-curtain airbags. This SKU range covers both the standard TJ and the LJ Unlimited.

Fitment Notes

  • The Unlimited (LJ) is a long-wheelbase 2-door only -- it does not have a 4-door body style (that came later with the JKU).
  • Most TJ/LJ trims have no side-curtain airbags.
  • If running aftermarket high-back bucket seats, none of the covers above will fit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best seat covers for a 2003-2006 TJ or LJ Unlimited?

Bartact is the top overall pick for this SKU range. Rough Country's TJ/LJ-specific covers (853 reviews, 4.7 stars) are the best Amazon alternative.

What's the difference between the TJ and the LJ Unlimited?

The LJ Unlimited (2004-2006) is a long-wheelbase, 2-door-only variant of the TJ with more rear legroom and cargo space. Front seat shape and cover fitment are the same as the standard TJ.

Will 1997-2002 TJ covers fit a 2003-2006 TJ?

Generally yes for the front seats since the shape is similar, but always check the specific product's listed year range -- some manufacturers split their patterns exactly at 2003.
