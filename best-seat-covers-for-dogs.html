What to Look For

  • Universal fit — covers with adjustable straps and seat belt pass-throughs work in most vehicles without vehicle-specific sizing
  • Waterproof fabric — look for TPU-coated 600D Oxford or 4-layer waterproof construction; avoid cheap single-layer polyester
  • Non-slip backing — keeps the cover from bunching up under an active dog
  • Hammock vs. bench — hammock style prevents dogs from falling into the foot well; bench style is flat and better for dogs that lie down
  • Side flaps — protect car doors from scratches and keep hair from getting into the gaps
