The Bottom Line on Universal Covers

Universal covers are a reasonable solution when a custom-fit option doesn't exist for your vehicle. But if you drive a Jeep, Bronco, Tacoma, 4Runner, Gladiator, or most popular trucks — custom-fit covers exist, and they're dramatically better.
