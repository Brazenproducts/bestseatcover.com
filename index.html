Jeep Wrangler JL & JLU Seat Covers (2018–Present)

The JL generation (2018–present) is the most configuration-sensitive Wrangler for seat covers. Three things to nail before you order:

  • Body style: 2-door JL or 4-door JLU — front covers are the same, rear covers are different
  • Trim: Mojave and 392 have unique front seats — covers listed as "not for Mojave/392" will not fit correctly
  • Year: 2024+ JL introduced optional power seats — if your Wrangler has power seat controls on the side bolster, confirm power-seat compatibility before ordering
⚠ 2024+ owners: The 2024 model year changed the seat back on power-seat-equipped JLs. Most covers from 2023 and earlier will fit if your seats are manual. If you have power seats, look for the 2024+ specific SKU.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will JK seat covers fit a JL Wrangler?

No. The JL and JK have completely different seat pan geometry. Always buy covers made specifically for your generation.

Do JL and JLU have the same seat covers?

Front seat covers are the same. Rear covers differ — the JLU 4-door rear bench is longer and shaped differently than the JL 2-door. Order body-style-specific rear covers.

What's the best material for Jeep seat covers?

For hard off-road use: heavy-duty nylon (Bartact's 1000D Cordura nylon) — durable, doesn't hold moisture, resists abrasion from gear and dogs. For water and beach use: neoprene (Smittybilt, Wet Okole) — waterproof and flexible. Polyester covers work for light use but wear faster.

Do Mojave and 392 Wranglers need different covers?

Yes. Both trims have unique front seats. Most generic "fits JL" covers explicitly exclude Mojave and 392. Bartact makes Mojave/392-specific SKUs — confirm this when ordering from any brand.

Do 2024+ JL Wranglers need different seat covers?

Only if you have power seats. If your 2024+ has manual seats (most Sport, Sport S, and Willys trims), pre-2024 covers generally fit. Power seat owners need the 2024+ specific SKU — check the side bolster for an adjustment switch.

Are Bartact seat covers worth the price?

If you actually use your Jeep off-road, yes. Bartact covers are custom-cut for your exact trim and body style, built from 1000D Cordura nylon (the same fabric in military gear), made in the USA, and come with MOLLE panels for mounting gear on the seat backs. Nothing else at any price point offers the same combination of fitment accuracy and build quality for Jeep use.

What's the difference between Bartact Tactical and Base Line Performance?

Tactical: full MOLLE grid on seat backs, wider color/pattern selection, higher price. Base Line Performance: solid colors only, no MOLLE panels, lower price point but same Cordura material and custom-cut fitment. Both are made in the USA.
