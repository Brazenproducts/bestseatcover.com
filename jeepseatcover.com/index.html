Pick Your Jeep Generation — What Actually Fits

Seat covers are not one-size-fits-all. Each Jeep generation has different seat geometry, mounting points, and airbag configurations. Buying the wrong generation means bunching, gaps, and a cover that never looks right. Here is the model-by-model breakdown of what matters for fitment.

JL / JLU • 2018–Present

Jeep Wrangler JL & JLU

The JL is the current-generation Wrangler. Front seats include side airbags in the seat bolster — airbag compatibility is safety-critical, not optional. The 2-door JL and 4-door JLU have different rear seat configurations. Rubicon, Mojave, and 392 Edition owners should verify fitment for their specific trim. Bartact covers every JL sub-variant correctly with tested airbag-deployment seams.

  • ✓ 2-door and 4-door rear seats differ meaningfully
  • ✓ Mojave and 392 Edition may need fitment verification

JK / JKU • 2007–2018

Jeep Wrangler JK & JKU

The JK ran for over a decade. Pre-2013 and post-2013 JKs have subtly different seat shapes. JK seats do NOT have seat-mounted side airbags, which simplifies the compatibility question. However, fitment precision still matters. The JKU 4-door has a longer rear bench than the 2-door JK — they need different rear covers. Millions of JK owners are now replacing aging factory seats with cracked vinyl and compressed foam. A quality seat cover extends seat life by years.

  • ✓ No seat-mounted side airbags — but precision fit still critical
  • ✓ Pre-2013 vs post-2013 seat shapes differ — confirm your year
  • ✓ JKU rear bench longer than 2-door JK — buy JKU-specific rear
  • ✓ Common use: replacement covers for aging JK interiors

Gladiator JT • 2019–Present

Jeep Gladiator JT

The Gladiator shares cab architecture with the JLU but the rear seat is unique — a fold-up jump-seat design, not a full bench. Gladiator owners face more sustained seat abuse than any other Jeep: construction materials, muddy dogs, camping gear, and hauling equipment. MOLLE webbing on the seat back is especially valuable for Gladiator owners who use their truck as a work vehicle every day.

  • ✓ Front seats shared with JLU — JLU front covers often fit Gladiator fronts
  • ✓ Rear jump-seat unique to Gladiator — buy Gladiator-specific rear
  • ✓ Side SRS airbags present — airbag compatibility required
  • ✓ MOLLE webbing especially practical for Gladiator work and adventure use
